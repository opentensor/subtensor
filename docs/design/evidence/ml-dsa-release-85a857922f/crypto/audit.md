## Existing condition and assurance limits

The previously reported unregistered-address association condition remains in current source and has an explicit runtime regression demonstration at `runtime/src/mldsa_dispatch_matrix_tests.rs:410`. An attacker need not guess an undisclosed hash: after a bare funding transfer discloses the target, a weak hotkey association can block later MS registration. The genuine key alone then cannot spend via the missing registry record. The register-and-finalize-before-public-disclosure/funding workflow avoids this condition; an atomic descriptor-aware registration/payment fails without funding when registration fails. This is not newly classified as a primitive cryptographic defect, and it must not be described as unconditional recovery for any bare prefunding sequence.

The administrative toggle gates direct protected signing, registration and capability use while preserving protection and prior state. Existing completed/composed authorizations require distinct semantics: the reviewed pause test does not establish that an already approved mixed multisig cannot be completed by a classical final signer while paused. No claim of universal revocation is made, and this review made no policy change.

The 32-byte ledger AccountId has an approximately 128-bit generic quantum targeted-preimage bound; the system is not uniformly category 3. Master compromise reveals future derived keys. Complete erasure of temporary KDF/hash state and Python-exported secret copies is not established. No CPU/cache/power/fault or compiler-level constant-time guarantee was tested. Browser/Wasm MS signing and physical hardware-wallet support are not established by native interoperability. A metadata-call matrix is envelope/dispatch coverage, not proof of every successful application-state transition.

The official [NIST FIPS 204 page](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/204/final) was rechecked on October 18 and still points to the July 31, 2026 potential-update errata reviewed in the preceding audit. Its relevant formatted-message, hint range, Montgomery arithmetic and bounded-signing-attempt corrections remain addressed as described there; no new implementation change was indicated by that check.
