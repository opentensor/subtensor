The requested additional debt needs more collateral under the market-depth, LTV or funded-redemption checks. Quote lending_required_collateral for this amount. Insufficient wallet funds, unavailable inventory and other loans' uncovered debt are separate refusals; the failed call moves no assets.

Declared by the `Lending` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`insufficient_balance`](/docs/errors/insufficient-balance).

Declared at [`pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L605`](/code/pallets/lending/src/lib.rs#L605).

## Remediation

Fund the signing account or reduce the amount; check with `btcli wallet balance`

The same explanation is available in the terminal: `btcli explain InsufficientCollateral`.
