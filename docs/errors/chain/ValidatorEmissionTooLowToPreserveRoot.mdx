Slot 3 (validator) of the emission weight vector is too low to keep root whole. Changing the validator share makes the chain re-solve this subnet's effective TAO weight once, at this block, so root holders keep exactly their current share of the subnet's emission; that weight is capped at 1 (root TAO never counts more than 1:1). The requested share is at or below root's current share, or would need a weight above 1. The floor is `r * (1 + alpha_issuance / root_tao)` where `r` is root's current share; `btcli subnets emission show --netuid <n>` prints it for this block. Raise the validator share and resubmit.

Declared by the `SubtensorModule` pallet; it classifies to the semantic code [`policy_violation`](/docs/errors/policy-violation).
